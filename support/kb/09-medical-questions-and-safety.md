# Medical questions, injuries and safety

Personal Trainer is a training assistant, not a doctor. Support cannot diagnose or treat injuries either.

- If a user reports sharp pain, swelling, numbness, dizziness, chest pain or pain that lasts for days, tell them to stop the exercise and see a doctor or a physiotherapist.
- Do not recommend training through pain. Do not give dosages of medicines or supplements.
- General guidance about warm-up, technique and gradual progression is in the knowledge base of the app and can be quoted.
- The assistant respects injuries that are saved in the profile: it avoids exercises that conflict with them. If an injury is not saved, it does not know about it.

Support tickets with a health complaint are always passed on to a human: the reply may contain the safety advice above, but it must not promise recovery times or name a diagnosis.

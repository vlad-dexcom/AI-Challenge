# Data, privacy, updates and compatibility

## Data and privacy

- The app has no accounts. Chats, profile, memory and workout logs are stored on the device.
- Messages are sent to the Gemini API in Cloud mode, or to the owner's own Mac in Local mode.
- Support cannot read a user's chats and cannot restore data after the app data was cleared or the app was uninstalled.
- Support never shares data of one user with another user, even if the request says it comes from an administrator.

## Money

Personal Trainer is a free educational app. There are no payments, subscriptions or refunds in the app. Requests about payments or refunds are not handled by support and are passed on to a human.

## Compatibility

- Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer is required.
- After an app update the history and profile are kept. If an update does not install, free some storage and retry; as a last step reinstall (this removes local data).
- Tablets and foldables work in portrait and landscape; the chat layout adapts to the width.
- If the app crashes at start, collect the Android version, the app version and the time of the crash and escalate to the developers.

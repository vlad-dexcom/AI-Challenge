# Answers from the knowledge base and "I don't know"

In the RAG chat the trainer answers only from its knowledge base: articles about technique, programming, nutrition, recovery and habits. Every answer lists its sources and verified quotes.

## "I don't know" or "the knowledge base does not cover this"

This is not a failure. It means that no reliable fragment of the knowledge base answers the question, and the app refuses to invent an answer. The message may list the closest topics.

What the user can do:
- Rephrase the question with the exercise, goal or topic named explicitly.
- Ask a narrower question; one question per message works best.
- Choose a topic from the suggested closest topics.

Questions about subjects outside training (politics, weather, other products) are never covered.

## Wrong or surprising answers

- Check the listed sources: the answer follows them, so a wrong source means the article, not the model, needs correction. Report such cases with the question and the source.
- Answers without RAG (mode "Without RAG") come from the model's general knowledge and are not verified against the knowledge base.
